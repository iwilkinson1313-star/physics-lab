Why radians?

One radian is the angle subtended when the arc length equals the radius. Angle in radians = arc length ÷ radius. Since a circle has circumference 2πr, one full turn is 2π radians = 360°.

Phase identifies a position within a cycle.

At π radians you are halfway through; at 2π you have completed a cycle. The same displacement can occur at different phases: phase is not the same as displacement.

Constructive and destructive interference are special cases.

For equal-amplitude waves, phase differences of 0, 2π, 4π… give maximum constructive interference; π, 3π, 5π… give complete destructive interference. Other phase differences give intermediate resultant amplitudes.
