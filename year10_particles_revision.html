2 · Answer-chain challenges

Try writing 3–5 connected scientific statements before revealing the prompt. The prompts are not full model answers.

1. Why does a gas exert pressure on the walls of its container?

Particle motion → collisions with walls → force on walls → pressure.

2. Why does heating a sealed rigid container of gas increase its pressure?

Temperature → average kinetic energy → faster collisions and greater momentum change → greater force per area.

3. Why does gas pressure fall when volume increases at constant temperature?

Greater space → fewer wall collisions per second per unit area → lower pressure.

4. Why are gases generally less dense than liquids?

For a given mass, more widely spaced particles occupy a greater volume.

5. Why does the temperature remain constant as ice melts?

Energy increases particle potential energy as attractive forces are overcome; average kinetic energy stays constant.

6. Why can equal masses given the same energy have different temperature rises?

Compare specific heat capacities and use ΔE = mcΔθ.

7. Why does a hot object initially cool more quickly?

Larger temperature difference with surroundings → faster energy transfer.

8. Why does insulating a hot-water tank save money?

Less energy transferred to surroundings → heater needed less often → less electricity purchased.

9. Why can rapid compression warm air in a bicycle pump?

Work done on gas transfers energy → internal energy can rise → temperature rises.

10. Why does halving a trapped gas volume double pressure at constant temperature?

Wall collisions become more frequent per unit area; pV stays constant.

Answer: 2700 kg/m³

Answer: 2.0 kg

Answer: 27,000 J

5. A 0.50 kg block gains 6000 J and warms by 20°C. Find c.

Answer: 600 J/kg°C
SHC

Answer: 12,432,000 J
Latent heat

Answer: 83,500 J
Latent heat

Answer: 300,000 J/kg
Multistage

Answer: 71,000 J
Gas laws

10. Gas: 100 kPa, 60 cm³ → 40 cm³ at constant temperature. Find new pressure.

Answer: 150 kPa
Gas laws

11. Gas: 200 cm³ at 150 kPa → 100 kPa. Find new volume.

Answer: 300 cm³
Gas laws
